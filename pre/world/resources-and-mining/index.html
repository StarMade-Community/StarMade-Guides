Resources & Mining

Raw resources are the foundation of everything you build. There are four families of raw materials, each found in different places across the universe.

Resource Types​

TypeAppearanceFound On
OresColoured rock chunksAsteroids, planet surfaces
Crystal ShardsGlittering crystalsAsteroids, planet surfaces
GasesColourful wispsGas Giant atmospheres, Nebulae
Exotic MaterialsVariousVarious

Each resource type has a different colour and name (e.g. Threns Ore, Varat Shards). For basic crafting, the specific type matters less than whether it is an ore or a shard — you just need enough of both to start.

Where to Find Resources​

Asteroids​

Asteroid fields appear as clusters of irregular objects in space. Asteroids contain pockets of Ores and Crystal Shards on their surfaces and throughout their interiors, identifiable by colour.

Nebulae​

Nebulae are massive coloured clouds of plasma, covering several star systems. Depending on the nebula type, ships within nebulae may experience various adverse effects, but will not be directly damaged. Gaseous resources can be harvested from Nebulae using a Gas Harvester.

Space Stations within Nebulae can also accumulate resources passively from them using an Ambient Gas Extractor.

Gas Planets​

Gas Planets, a.k.a. Gas Giants, are massive planets with turbulent atmospheres and no reachable solid surface. Gaseous and sometimes exotic resources can be harvested from the proximity of Gas Giants using a Gas Harvester.

Hot Gas Giants are superheated to such an extreme degree that their atmospheres contain vaporized forms of solid resources, which can also be harvested the same way.

Space Stations in/adjacent to Gas Planet sectors can also accumulate resources passively from some Gas Giants using an Ambient Gas Extractor.

Terrestrial Planets​

Planet surfaces contain Ore and Shard deposits far more concentrated than those in Asteroids, but they cannot be mined by ships' salvage systems. Astronauts exploring a planet's surface may even strike lucky veins, containing even more of a resource than usual.

Bases built on Terrestrial Planets can also accumulate solid resources passively from some planets using a Deep Core Extractor.

Wreckage​

A ship that is Overheating, or any unclaimed Space Station, can be harvested for blocks via the Salvage Beam. Any blocks obtained from wrecks can be used as-is or broken down into valuable Components via the Block Recycler factory.

Ancient, abandoned, & decayed stations can be occasionally found floating throughout the cosmos, however upon salvage their blocks only yield Scrap Metal and Scrap Composite that must be restored to a usable state via the Micro Reprocessor.

How to Mine​

As an Astronaut​

  1. Hold the right mouse button while looking at a resource block. Your character fires a mining beam.
  2. The block breaks after a short duration and drops items into your inventory automatically.
  3. Make sure no weapon is selected (scroll past the last weapon slot or press the mouse wheel to deselect).

With a Ship​

To harvest solids & blocks, build a Salvage Computer and Salvage Beam Modules on your ship (same setup as weapons). Aim the salvage beam at asteroid, abandoned station, or wrecked (Overheating) ship to harvest them rapidly. Ships can mine much faster than astronaut mode and collect materials directly into the ship's storage.

To harvest gases, build a Gas Harvester Computer and Gas Harvester Modules on your ship, likewise. Activating this module will begin a cycle. As long as the system is supplied with power for the entire cycle and there are resource traces present in the area, you will receive collected resources at the end.

With an Extractor base​

Some locations throughout the galaxy have resources available to passively extract. Information about these locations can be obtained via the Long Range Scanner, with certain Recon Chamber upgrades.

Once you have found a passive resource node, resources can be harvested using the appropriate extractor. Nebulae and Gas Giant planets can be harvested using the Ambient Gas Extractor on a Space Station nearby, whereas terrestrial planets require the Deep Core Extractor. Connecting Factory Enhancers to your Extractor will increase its potential resource extraction rate, however each node has a maximum amount of resources that can be extracted per cycle regardless of enhancers.

Multiple passive extractors draining from one source node will split harvested resources amongst themselves proportionate to the amount of enhancers connected to them. Extraction will continue even while the structure is unloaded.

Processing Raw Materials​

Resources in their raw form cannot be used directly — they must be processed into capsules/Mesh/Composite first, then into manufacturing Components. Those Components can then be used to create blocks, ships, & stations.

See Crafting Basics for the full processing chain.

Both Metal Mesh and Crystal Circuit are the base ingredients for nearly every craftable block in the game.
