HUD & Interface

The HUD (Heads-Up Display) gives you live information about your character and ship at a glance.

Astronaut HUD​

On foot you see:

  • Crosshair in the centre of the screen.
  • Hotbar at the bottom, with your health bar (green) just above it.
  • Radar in the top-right corner, with your current sector coordinates below it. Use these when setting navigation waypoints.
  • Target panel in the top-left corner while you have something selected (F selects what you are looking at).

On a planet, a location readout under the radar adds your position on the planet, altitude, biome, time of day and the current weather.

Ship HUD​

When you fly a ship, bars curve around the crosshair:

SideBarWhat it shows
LeftShield (yellow)Shield charge
LeftArmor / StructureArmor HP and structure HP
RightPower (blue)Reactor power: what you have against what your systems are drawing
RightSpeed (white)Current speed
RightHeatHeat from cannon and beam fire. Heat Sink blocks soak it up; once they are full, those weapons can't fire until it cools.

Buffs (green) and debuffs (red) from weapon effects show up near the centre of the screen. A flash on the crosshair tells you what you hit: blue for shields, white for blocks, red for a character.

Hotbar​

Ten slots mapped to keys 1–9 and 0. The mouse wheel cycles slots (except in advanced build mode). On a slot with several block shapes, the wheel steps through those first. Holds:

  • Items and blocks (astronaut mode)
  • Weapons and systems (ship flight mode)
  • Blocks to place (build mode)

Press F2 to free the mouse cursor without opening a panel. All keys can be changed in the options.

Control Helpers​

Hints for the controls you can use right now appear on screen and change with what you are doing (on foot, flying, or building). Press / (default) to show or hide the full help screen.
