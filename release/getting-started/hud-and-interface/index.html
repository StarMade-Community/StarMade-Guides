HUD & Interface

The HUD (Heads-Up Display) gives you live information about your character and ship at a glance.

Astronaut HUD​

Key HUD Elements​

Status Bars (bottom-left)​

BarColourWhat it shows
HPRedYour character's or ship's hull health
ShieldBlueShield charge level
EnergyGreenAvailable energy for weapons and tools

Sector Coordinates (top-centre)​

Shows your current sector position as [X, Y, Z] next to your player name. Use these when setting navigation waypoints.

Hotbar (bottom-centre)​

Ten slots mapped to keys 1–9 and 0. Scroll the mouse wheel to cycle selection. Holds:

  • Items and blocks (astronaut mode)
  • Weapons assigned in the weapon panel (ship flight mode)
  • Build tools (ship build mode)

Panels & Menus​

PanelDefault KeyDescription
InventoryIPersonal inventory and personal crafting
NavigationNNearby objects, filters, and waypoints
CatalogVia Inventory tabSaved ship blueprints
Weapon PanelG (in ship)Assign and manage ship weapons
Structure PanelDelete (in ship)Manage docked entities and AI
Faction MenuHFaction management
ChatEnterOpen chat; prefix /f for faction chat

Control Helpers​

Press / (default) in-game to see a context-sensitive control reference that changes depending on whether you are in astronaut mode, flight mode, or build mode.
