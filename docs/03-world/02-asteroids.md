# Asteroids

![asteroids_fig1.jpg](https://web.archive.org/web/20241010090719im_/https://starmadepedia.net/images/6/61/Asteroids.jpg)

Asteroids are massive boulders found in space orbiting stars. They are a standard source of valuable Ores and Crystals needed for building, as well as bulk stone materials. They are harvested with ship-based Salvage Systems.
Each star system's asteroids has a different rock composition, but any color of asteroid can contain the same resources.

### Where To Find Them

Asteroids can occur throughout star-systems, but are primarily concentrated within Asteroid Belts. These are marked in yellow on your Map.

![asteroids_fig2.jpg](https://web.archive.org/web/20241010090719im_/https://starmadepedia.net/images/c/cd/Asteroid_belt_orbits.jpg)

## Interaction

The primary utility of Asteroids is typically as a mining resource. Asteroids contain various types of embedded Ores, dependent upon what resources are overall common in a Star System.
Asteroids may bear metallic or crystalline Ore types, but do *NOT* contain Gases, which must instead be harvested by other means.

Asteroids can be claimed, built on, and used as a base, similar to a Space Station. However, it is impossible to save blueprints of an Asteroid, and you cannot set an Asteroid as a Home Base or use it to lay claim to a Star System.

If collision damage is turned on, you may consider throwing asteroids at your enemies using Tractor Beams!
